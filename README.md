# worldz

A country guessing game. You're given a country, you click it on the map.

Same look as [Pebblez](https://github.com/JohnFletcherSnodgrass/pebblez): coral, sun yellow, navy ink, Caveat handwriting. Night mode is the default (dark map); light mode is one tap away.

## Play

No build step. Serve the folder and open it:

```bash
npm start        # python3 -m http.server 8080, then open http://localhost:8080
```

(Or open `index.html` directly; everything is local.)

- **Map:** World, Africa, Asia, Europe, North America, South America, Oceania.
- **Countries:** All (default), Small (under 100,000 km²) or Big (100,000 km² and up).
- **Difficulty:**
  | Level  | Found countries fill in + get labelled | Guesses per country |
  | ------ | :------------------------------------: | :-----------------: |
  | Easy (default) | yes | 3 |
  | Medium | yes | 1 |
  | Hard   | no  | 3 |
  | Expert | no  | 1 |
- The country to find is printed in a small orange label under the mouse pointer. Press **F** (or the button) for full screen, **S** to skip, scroll or pinch to zoom, drag to pan. The map can't be dragged past its own edge.
- A right answer turns green: brightest on the first guess, darker on the second, darkest on the third. Missed countries turn red.
- A wrong guess leaves the label under the pointer alone. The country you clicked shows its name and shakes.
- Countries too small to see at the current zoom show as clickable dots; zoom in and they turn into their real outline (Tuvalu has no outline in the map data, so it always stays a dot).

### Hints

A **Hints** dropdown, in the menu and in the game's top bar, switches each of these on or off:

| Hint | What it does | Default |
| ---- | ------------ | :-----: |
| Direction arrow | After a wrong guess, points from your click toward the answer and shows the distance (3-guess levels) | on |
| Neighbour outline | On your last guess, outlines the countries that border the answer (3-guess levels) | on |
| Fly to the answer | On a miss, zooms to the country, then back | on |
| Missed countries come back | Asked again later in the round; score keeps the first try | off |
| Region tints | A faint colour per sub-region | off |
| Dots for small countries | Tiny countries are clickable dots until you zoom in (key **D**) | on |
| Streak and best scores | Streak while playing; best score and time per map, saved in your browser | on |
| Pop when you get one right | Small animation on a right answer | on |
| Paper grain | Light texture on the map | on |
| Flags on the results screen | Flags next to missed countries | on |

The results screen has a **Practice these** button that plays just the countries you missed.
- The map is drawn on a canvas (not SVG) to stay smooth. If frames come out slow it lowers its drawing resolution automatically.
- On a continent map you start locked on that continent, but you can zoom out and pan around the whole world. Only countries in the chosen map and size are clickable.

## Data (all real, nothing drawn by hand)

`data/world-data.js` is generated by `npm run build:data` from:

- [`world-atlas`](https://github.com/topojson/world-atlas): Natural Earth 1:50m country outlines (public domain).
- [`world-countries`](https://github.com/mledoze/countries): names, regions, sub-regions, flags, coordinates and areas (ODbL).

Land borders for the neighbour hint come from the atlas topology (shapes that share an edge), not from a hand-made list.

The build script checks every outline's area against the listed area to catch mismatches. Choices that are editorial rather than data are marked `EDITORIAL` in `scripts/build-data.mjs`: independent states plus Taiwan, Palestine and Kosovo (197 total); Russia is playable on both the Europe and Asia maps and Turkey on both Asia and Europe; Somaliland is drawn as part of Somalia and Northern Cyprus as part of Cyprus. Tuvalu has no outline at this resolution, so it's a dot.

Map drawing uses [d3](https://d3js.org) (ISC), vendored in `vendor/`.
